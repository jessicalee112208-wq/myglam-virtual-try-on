# How to Use MyGlam Virtual Try-On Clothes

MyGlam Virtual Try-On Clothes lets you preview different outfits on your own photo using AI. You only need a person photo and a clothing image to get started.

## Step 1: Upload Your Photo

Choose a clear, full-body or upper-body photo.

For better virtual try-on results:

- Use a front-facing photo
- Make sure your body is clearly visible
- Use good natural lighting
- Avoid heavily filtered photos
- Avoid clothing that completely hides your body shape

## Step 2: Upload a Clothing Image

Next, upload the clothing item you would like to try on.

You can experiment with different styles, including:

- Tops
- Shirts
- Jackets
- Dresses
- Pants
- Casual outfits
- Business outfits

For best results, use a clear product photo where the garment is easy to see.

## Step 3: Generate Your Virtual Try-On

After uploading both images, start the AI virtual try-on.

MyGlam analyzes the person photo and clothing reference to create a new preview showing how the selected outfit may look on the person.

Generation usually takes only a short time.

## Step 4: Compare Different Outfit Ideas

You can repeat the process with different clothing images to explore multiple looks.

This can be useful when:

- Comparing outfits before shopping
- Exploring a new fashion style
- Planning an outfit
- Creating fashion content
- Previewing clothing combinations
